## 2. Core Privacy Architecture: Local-First Data Storage

PuffinLess is engineered around a fundamental principle of **data minimization and personal sovereignty**:

### 2.1 What Stays on Your Device
All your personal addiction recovery records, including:
- Clean breath start timestamps and streak records;
- Daily counts of consumed cigarettes, vape puffs, or cannabis joints;
- Situational triggers (Stress, After Meal, Social, Alcohol, Boredom);
- Resisted cravings and urge surfing scores;
- Sleep quality ratings (1–10) and energy ratings (1–10);
- Personal financial expenses and dream reward goals;
- Security access PINs (hashed locally);

**are stored directly and exclusively within your device's private local browser storage.**

### 2.2 Zero Central Habit Database
The Publisher does **NOT** operate, maintain, or connect PuffinLess to a central remote database storing your personal habit history. Your records are not uploaded, synchronized, or duplicated onto our servers.

## 5. User Control, Data Portability & Irreversible Deletion

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Articles 15, 17, and 20) and equivalent global privacy laws, you have full sovereignty over your information:

### 5.1 Right to Access & Data Portability (Article 20 GDPR)
You can export a complete, machine-readable copy of your entire history at any time:
1. Open **Settings** (Profile modal).
2. Scroll to **Data Backup & Storage Management**.
3. Click **Export Backup (.json)**.  
A `.json` file containing your full uncorrupted history will download directly to your local file system.

### 5.2 Right to Erasure / Immediate Deletion (Article 17 GDPR)
You can permanently erase all records from your active browser profile:
1. Open **Settings** -> **Local Reset & Data Deletion**.
2. Click **Erase All My Data** and confirm the prompt.
3. The application immediately destroys all local storage records, event journals, and active sessions within that browser profile.

> **Important Scope Note:** Resetting your browser profile does not delete backup files (`.json`) previously saved to your computer disk, nor does it affect copies stored in other browsers or on other devices. Those must be deleted manually by you.
