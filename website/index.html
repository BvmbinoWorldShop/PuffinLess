Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my personal records stored? ▼

Your data is stored directly in your browser's physical memory using IndexedDB and localStorage. PuffinLess operates on a local-first architecture: no personal habit logs or consumption entries are uploaded to cloud servers.

How do I move my records between my phone and computer? ▼

Because there is no automatic central cloud sync, moving records between devices is done in seconds: click "Export" in Settings to download your backup file (.json), and click "Import" on your second device. You remain in complete control of your files.

Does PuffinLess work without internet access? ▼

Yes! PuffinLess is 100% offline-first. You can install it as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on iOS Safari or Android Chrome, or open the standalone single-file HTML directly. It works in airplane mode, subway tunnels, and remote retreats.

Is PuffinLess a medical service? ▼

No. PuffinLess is a personal habit tracker and self-monitoring companion. It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment, or clinical prescriptions. If you experience severe withdrawal symptoms or health complications, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Why is there no monthly subscription? ▼

Because we do not run expensive cloud server farms or track your personal data, we do not have large monthly hosting overhead to bill you for. You pay once for the software, and it is yours to use.
